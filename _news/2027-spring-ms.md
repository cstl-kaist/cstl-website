---
title: Recruiting MS students for Spring 2027
date: 2026-09-21
---

The Collaborative Social Technologies Lab is recruiting MS students for Spring 2027. Candidates must have already been admitted to the KAIST School of Computing or AI Computing Department for Spring 2027. Ideal candidates will have experience and interest with HCI and/or AI. If you are an interested candidate, please email Professor Joseph Seering (__seering@kaist.ac.kr__) by **Oct 9th (Fri) KST** with the following information:

- A copy of your CV and transcript.
- Any relevant courses you are currently taking that are not listed on your transcript.
- A one page statement of purpose (in English) detailing your research interests and relevant experience.

Application materials will be reviewed promptly, and interviews will be scheduled during the following week. Interviews will include a presentation of research interests to current graduate students and a 1-on-1 meeting with the professor. Notifications will be sent out by Oct 25th.
